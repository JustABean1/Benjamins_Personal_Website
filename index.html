About

I start with a vision, then build it until it's real, whether that's an interface, a poster or something you can hold. I fell for design through computer science and the first websites I built, and I've been sharpening the craft ever since. Paul Rand's clarity and Keith Haring's energy guide my eye, and music shapes everything else. Trying too hard shows, so I aim for work that's sleek, simple and good, and I treat every mistake as a step toward something I'm proud of.
